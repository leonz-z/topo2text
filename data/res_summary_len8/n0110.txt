This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Aitai Router, Aitai Switches, HiPER 510W access points, and client devices such as laptops and three white machines. The nodes are connected via switches and routers, facilitating both wired and wireless communication. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity, ensuring reliable access to external resources and services.
