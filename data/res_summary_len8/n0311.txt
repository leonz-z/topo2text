This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include servers (a server, b server, c server), a Data acquisition front-end processor, a Group VPN Private Network, a Data Collection Service, a Subject system, a Master database, and a Standby database. The connections are primarily facilitated through the Group VPN Private Network, which enables secure communication between different segments. The overall structure supports data collection, processing, and secure data transfer. Notable features include redundancy through the Standby database and integration of various data sources, ensuring efficient communication between the company and group entities.
