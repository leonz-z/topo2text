This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include TX-HomeFW (firewall), RuckusAP (wireless access point), Syrah, LOIRE, LSACE, and various WAN connections to locations like Boston and Atlanta. These nodes are connected via a combination of routers and switches, facilitating both local and wide-area communications.

The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy, ensuring connectivity across different geographical locations. Notable features include the integration of multiple connection types, enhancing scalability and security, while enabling effective communication between local networks and external resources.
