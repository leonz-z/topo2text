This network topology represents a **hybrid topology** architecture. Key components include a **专网交换机 (Dedicated Network Switch)**, **防火墙 (Firewall)**, **电信路由器 (Telecom Router)**, **机房局域网交换机 (Data Center LAN Switch)**, **日志统计系统 (Log Statistics System)**, **国资系统 (State-owned Assets System)**, **中控系统 (Central Control System)**, and **电信终端 (Telecom Terminal)**, connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports secure and efficient communication between various internal systems and external networks. Notable features include enhanced security through the firewall and centralized control via the dedicated switch, ensuring reliable network operations and data management.
