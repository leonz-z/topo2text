This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **Core Switches, Internal BN, EN pairs, WLCs (Wireless LAN Controllers), CNs (Control Nodes), External BN, Fabric Site, and EN (Edge Nodes)**, connected via multiple paths allowing for redundancy and efficient data flow. The overall structure supports **robust communication between internal and external networks**, ensuring high availability and scalability. Notable features include **traffic management and interconnectivity**, facilitating seamless connectivity for user devices and network services.
