This network topology represents a star topology architecture. Key components include 中国电信 (China Telecom) as the internet service provider, a main switch, distribution switches, fiber optic splitters, and various end devices such as computers. The connections are primarily facilitated through switches and fiber optic links. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between internal devices and the external internet. Notable features include redundancy through multiple switches and fiber optic connections, ensuring high availability and scalability, which enhances communication between user devices and external networks.
