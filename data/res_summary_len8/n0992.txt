This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include servers, a router, a firewall, hub switches, a Wi-Fi router, and various client devices such as laptops, mobile phones, tablets, and printers. The devices are connected via wired and wireless connections, facilitating communication across the network. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing while enhancing security through the firewall. Notable features include organized network segments for servers and clients, ensuring scalability and robust access to services.
