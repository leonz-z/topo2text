This network topology represents a **hybrid topology**. Key components include **Cloud1, FW1 (Firewall), R (Router), AR1 (Access Router), S1 (Access Switch), S2 (Access Switch), AC1 (Access Controller), AP1 (Access Point), and AP2 (Access Point)**, connected via **Gigabit Ethernet (GE) links**. The overall structure supports **secure communication and resource access** among various devices, with a focus on both wired and wireless connectivity. Notable features include **network segmentation and security measures**, ensuring efficient communication between **end devices (STA1 and STA2) and the core network infrastructure**.
