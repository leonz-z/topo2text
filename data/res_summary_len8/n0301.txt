This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include two 2950-24 Switches and multiple PCs connected across three VLANs (VLAN 1, VLAN 2, and VLAN 3). The switches connect the PCs, facilitating communication within the same VLAN and between different VLANs. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and network segmentation for improved performance and security.
