This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include NAS/Ceph/Local Storage, application servers, transcoding servers, database servers, switches, routers, firewalls, and client devices. The connections are primarily facilitated through switches and routers, allowing for both centralized and distributed communication. The overall structure supports efficient data management, application processing, and secure communication. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring reliable access for both enterprise and individual users.
