This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a router, a firewall, three hub switches, servers, a printer, and client devices. The components are connected via Ethernet cables through the hub switches, facilitating communication within the local area network (LAN). The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among users. Notable features include centralized management of network traffic and security, ensuring reliable communication between clients and servers, as well as access to shared resources like the printer.
