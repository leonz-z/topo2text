This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include star segments connecting multiple computers to a central hub or switch, and a ring segment where computers are connected in a circular fashion. The connections are primarily facilitated by switches and a central hub, managing data traffic between the segments. The overall structure supports efficient communication and enhances network performance by combining the strengths of both star and ring topologies. Notable features include flexibility and scalability, ensuring reliable data flow between various user groups and services.
