This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the regenerative satellite, distribution nodes (DN 1, DN 2, DN N), and various access networks (DVB-T, WLAN, and other broadband technologies). These components are connected via satellite links and local distribution nodes. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and content delivery to end-users. Notable features include redundancy through multiple distribution nodes and diverse access technologies, ensuring reliable communication between users and content providers.
