This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the GW RTR NOC, Classroom switch, routers R11, R12, R61, R62, and rtr1, as well as various switches (SW11, SW12, SW13, SW14, SW61, SW62, SW63, SW64) and access point ap1. The connections are primarily facilitated through switches and routers, creating a structured communication pathway. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between classroom devices, desktop PCs, and external networks. Notable features include redundancy and segmentation, ensuring efficient communication between user groups and services within the classroom and administrative environments.
