This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Amazon Route 53 Hosted Zone, Elastic Load Balancer, Amazon EC2 Instances, Auto Scaling Group, App Tier, ElasticCache Tier, Amazon RDS, and Amazon S3 Bucket, connected via various services and protocols.
