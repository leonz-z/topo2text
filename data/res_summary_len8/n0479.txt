This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, a 2811 router for internet access, a central Firewall, and multiple routers connected to various subnets (192.168.0.0/26, 192.168.1.0/24, 192.168.2.0/24, 1.1.1.0/24, 10.0.100.0/24, 172.31.128.0/20, 172.16.123.0/25, and 10.254.1.0/24). The connections are primarily facilitated through routers and the Firewall, which acts as a security barrier. The overall structure supports secure data flow and external connectivity. Notable features include a focus on security and internal communication, ensuring efficient interaction between internal devices and external resources.
