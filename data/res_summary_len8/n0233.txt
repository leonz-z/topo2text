This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include routers (R, AR1), Layer 2 switches (LSW6, LSW7, LSW8, LSW9, LSW10), and various VLAN segments (vlan20, vlan10, vlan30, vlan40). The nodes are connected via switches, facilitating communication across different segments. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and prioritization of traffic for specific departments, such as the CEO's office and finance department. Notable features include optimized traffic management and enhanced security measures, ensuring effective communication between user groups and services within the organization.
