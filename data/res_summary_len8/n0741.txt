This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the monitoring center (监控中心), hard disk recorder (硬盘录像机), core switch (干流交换机), PoE switch (PoE交换机), and network cameras (网络摄像机), connected via Ethernet and PoE connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and video surveillance. Notable features include centralized management and power delivery through PoE, ensuring reliable communication between surveillance devices and the monitoring system.
