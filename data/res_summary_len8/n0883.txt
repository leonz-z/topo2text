This network topology represents a **ring topology** architecture. Key components include **RN6000** (gateway to the Internet), **S4500 switches** (core switches managing data traffic), **万兆服务器 (10G Server)** (high-performance servers), and **终端客户机 (Client Devices)** (end-user devices). The connections are primarily facilitated through the S4500 switches, ensuring efficient data flow and resource sharing among client devices, servers, and external networks. Notable features include high availability and redundancy, ensuring reliable communication between user groups and services.
