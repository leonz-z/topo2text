This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include cameras, a switch, an NVR (Network Video Recorder), and a monitor. The devices are connected via Ethernet cables to the switch, which facilitates communication among them. The overall structure supports a surveillance system where cameras capture video, the switch manages data flow, the NVR records the footage, and the monitor displays it. Notable features include centralized management and ease of access, ensuring efficient communication between surveillance devices and user interfaces.
