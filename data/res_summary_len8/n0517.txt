This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **Intersite Network (ISN)**, **spine switches**, **leaf switches**, **Application Policy Infrastructure Controllers (APIC 1, APIC 2, APIC 3)**, and a **Multi-Site Orchestrator (MSO) Cluster**, connected via high-speed links between the spine and leaf layers, as well as connections to the ISN. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy between Data Center 1 and Data Center 2. Notable features include **high availability** and **scalability**, ensuring efficient communication between the data centers and their respective resources.
