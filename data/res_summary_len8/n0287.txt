This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the VoIP Service Provider, VoIP ATA, GoIP, GoIP4, GoIP8, Local GSM Network, Local Telephone Network, and the World Telephone Network, connected via the Internet/Intranet. The overall structure supports seamless voice communication across various platforms, bridging VoIP services with traditional telephony systems. Notable features include redundancy and integration capabilities, ensuring efficient communication between mobile devices, VoIP users, and standard telephone lines.
