This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include workstations for various departments (Product Design, Managing Director, Accounting, Sales, Shipping), multiple printers (Laser printer, Color printer, ASCII Dot Matrix printer), and a NETGEAR switch. The devices are connected via Ethernet cables to the central NETGEAR switch, facilitating communication and data transfer among them. The overall structure supports efficient communication, resource sharing, and collaboration among users in the network. Notable features include centralized management and enhanced performance, ensuring seamless interaction between all connected entities.
