This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the GQF630 gateway router, 核心交换机 (core switch), 服务器群 (server cluster), and multiple switches connecting various departments such as 财务部 (Finance Department), 加工部 (Processing Department), 机房 (Data Center), and 生活部 (Living Department). The connections are primarily facilitated through switches and VLANs, ensuring efficient communication and data flow. The overall structure supports organized network operations and departmental segmentation. Notable features include enhanced security through VLANs and the core switch's role in managing traffic, ensuring high availability and efficient communication between user groups.
