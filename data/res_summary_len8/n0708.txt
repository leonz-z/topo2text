This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Router, multiple Switches, a Server, a WiFi Router, IP Phones, and various PCs (both desktop and laptop). The connections are primarily facilitated through wired links between the Router, Switches, and Server, while wireless connectivity is provided by the WiFi Router for mobile devices. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication across different segments of the network. Notable features include a ring configuration for certain PCs, enhancing data management and reducing collisions, ensuring effective communication between users and services.
