This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the 主控中心 (Control Center), 交换机 (Switch), 光纤收发器 (Fiber Optic Transceiver), and various 摄像头 (Cameras), connected via switches and fiber optic transceivers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and surveillance management. Notable features include high availability and redundancy, ensuring reliable communication between the control center and connected devices.
