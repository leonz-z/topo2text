This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the AC3000-32 (Management Layer), 主路由 (Main Router), 核心交换机 (Core Switch), G3224T (Distribution Layer), and multiple G3210P and G3221P (Access Layer) switches. The nodes are connected via Gigabit optical fiber and copper cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between various network segments, ensuring reliable communication. Notable features include high availability and scalability, facilitating seamless access for end users through wireless access points (AP340, AP355).
