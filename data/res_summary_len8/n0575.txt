This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **ISP Modem**, **Router (Netgear)**, **Switch 1**, **Switch 2**, **Access Point 1**, and **Access Point 2**. These components are connected via **wired connections** through switches and **wireless connections** through access points. The overall structure supports **robust data flow and connectivity** across both wired and wireless devices. Notable features include **high availability and scalability**, ensuring efficient communication between various user devices such as computers, mobile devices, and printers.
