This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the IDC (Internet Data Center), S8505 and S8512 switches, and three campus segments: 宝山校区 (Baoshan Campus), 延长校区 (Yan Chang Campus), and 嘉定校区 (Jiading Campus). The nodes are connected via switches, facilitating efficient communication and data flow. The overall structure supports connectivity and resource sharing across the university's campuses. Notable features include centralized data management and redundancy, ensuring efficient communication between the various campus locations.
