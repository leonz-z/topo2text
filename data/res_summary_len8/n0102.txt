This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a central Router, client devices (laptops belonging to Joe, Sarah, Charlie, Monica, Nick, and Boss), an iPad for Reception DHCP, and a Printer Office. These components are connected via Ethernet cables to the Router, which facilitates communication between devices and manages traffic within the network. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource management. Notable features include the dynamic IP address allocation by the iPad and the dedicated printing services provided by the Printer Office, ensuring seamless communication between users and network resources.
