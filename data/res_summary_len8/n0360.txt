This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Firewall (WAF+IPS), multiple Switches (48Port and 24Port), Access Control (AC), and designated areas such as the Three-layer and Four-layer segments, along with large classrooms. These components are connected via both fiber optic and copper cabling. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security management within an educational institution. Notable features include a focus on security through the firewall and access control, ensuring effective communication between students, faculty, and network resources.
