This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Enterprise Level (ERP, PLM, WMS, BPM, PDM, 4C), Factory Level (MES and management modules), Process Level (SCADA and PLC), and the Electronic Platform. These elements are connected via various systems that facilitate data exchange and communication. The overall structure supports efficient operations, data management, and process control within a manufacturing environment. Notable features include a hierarchical organization that ensures strategic goals are translated into operational actions, with feedback loops for monitoring and optimization, ensuring effective communication between different levels of the network.
