This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include Layer 3 switches (L3) at the top layer, Layer 2 switches (L2) in the middle layer, and end devices such as servers and computers at the bottom layer. The nodes are connected via both Layer 2 and Layer 3 connections, facilitating communication across different segments of the network. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and inter-network communication. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring reliable connectivity between various user groups and services.
