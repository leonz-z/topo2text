This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Router, two Switches, a Server, and Wireless Access Points (WAPs) for both the IT and CSE departments. The Router connects the internal network to the Internet, facilitating communication between the departments and external resources. The Switches connect multiple devices within their respective segments, while the WAPs provide wireless connectivity for mobile devices. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and departmental networking. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring reliable communication between users and access to network resources.
