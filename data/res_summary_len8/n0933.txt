This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the BES Management Server, BES Server, BES Control nodes, Firewall, Modem Pool, BigFix Server, and Router. These are connected via a combination of direct links and centralized connections, facilitating both internal and external communication. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and management of enterprise resources. Notable features include security measures through the Firewall and redundancy via the BES Management Server, ensuring reliable communication between PCs in the Enterprise Network and Remote Office.
