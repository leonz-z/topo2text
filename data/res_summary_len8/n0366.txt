This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include R1-CE, R2-CE, R3-PE, R4-PE, R5-CE, and R6-CE, connected via GRE tunnels and Ethernet interfaces. The overall structure supports isolated routing domains (VRFs) and facilitates communication between customer and provider networks. Notable features include the use of VRF GREEN and VRF BLUE for traffic segmentation, ensuring efficient communication between various customer edge devices and provider edge routers.
