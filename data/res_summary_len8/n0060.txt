This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a central router (2811), multiple multilayer switches (3560-24PS, 3560-24PS1), access switches (2960-24T, 2950-24), and a wireless router (Linksys WRT300N). The devices are connected via Ethernet links, facilitating both wired and wireless communication. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among connected devices, ensuring reliable connectivity for end devices such as PCs and mobile devices. Notable features include inter-VLAN routing and redundancy, enhancing the network's performance and reliability.
