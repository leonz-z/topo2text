This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the MSC/MGW, GMSC, SGSN, GGSN, MME, HSS, Serving GW, and PDN GW, connected via various interfaces such as S1, S3, S4, and Gn. The overall structure supports mobile communication services across different generations of technology (2G, 3G, and 4G), ensuring efficient data flow and user mobility. Notable features include redundancy and session management capabilities, ensuring high availability and seamless transitions for user equipment across the network.
