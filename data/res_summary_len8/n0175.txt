This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a central router, a central switch (中心交换机), and five switch groups (交换机组 1 to 5), connected via UTP cables. The overall structure supports efficient communication within the internal network while providing access to external networks like CERNET and PSTN. Notable features include redundancy and centralized management, ensuring efficient communication between various user groups and services such as web hosting, email, and video on demand.
