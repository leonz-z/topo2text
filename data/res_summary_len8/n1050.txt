This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Data Center Switch, Router-Color and Router, multiple ATM/FastGb Etherswitches (1-5), and Cisco Unifi Presence Servers. These components are connected via Ethernet switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity among various devices and servers. Notable features include redundancy and high-speed data transfer capabilities, ensuring reliable communication between user groups and services within the network.
