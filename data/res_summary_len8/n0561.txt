This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include cameras (监控机), switches (交换机), routers (路由器), a digital matrix (数字矩阵), a management server (管理服务器), and an IPSAN (IPSAN). The devices are connected via switches, facilitating communication and data flow. The overall structure supports efficient surveillance and monitoring of video data. Notable features include centralized storage and management, ensuring effective monitoring and accessibility of video feeds for real-time analysis.
