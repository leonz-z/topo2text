This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a firewall, router, WiFi router, hub switch, servers 1 and 2, and various client devices. These components are connected via wired and wireless connections, facilitating communication within the local area network (LAN) and external access to the internet. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing while ensuring security. Notable features include a dedicated firewall for protection and a WiFi router for wireless connectivity, ensuring seamless communication between client devices and servers.
