This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, Firewall, multiple servers, and various buildings such as 教职工宿舍 (Staff Dormitory), 学生宿舍 (Student Dormitory), 图书馆 (Library), 办公楼 (Office Building), and 教学楼 (Teaching Building), all connected via a central switch. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between different user groups and services. Notable features include a centralized management point and enhanced security through the firewall, ensuring reliable connectivity and resource access across the network.
