This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include switches, routers, firewalls, web servers (Web服务器1 and Web服务器2), database servers (数据库服务器1 and 数据库服务器2), and a storage array. The connections are primarily facilitated through switches and routers, ensuring efficient data flow and communication. The overall structure supports high availability and load balancing, particularly in the web and database segments. Notable features include redundancy and security measures, ensuring efficient communication between end-user devices and the internal services.
