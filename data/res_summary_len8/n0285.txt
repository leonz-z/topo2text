This network topology represents a **hybrid topology** architecture. Key components include the **总控管理服务器 (Central Management Server)**, **流媒体服务器 (Streaming Media Server)**, **存储设备 (Storage Device)**, **交换机 (Switch)**, **高清矩阵 (HD Matrix)**, **光纤收发器 (Fiber Optic Transceiver)**, **警报求助 (Alarm Assistance)**, and **广播系统 (Broadcast System)**, connected via switches and fiber optic transceivers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication among various devices, ensuring robust media delivery and surveillance functionalities. Notable features include **data redundancy** and **security measures**, ensuring efficient communication between **client terminals** and **surveillance cameras**.
