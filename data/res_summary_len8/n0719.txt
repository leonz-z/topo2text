This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **POD 0**, **POD 1**, **POD 2**, and **POD 3**, connected via **10 Gbps** links in the core layer and **1 Gbps** links in the aggregation and edge layers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy across the network. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring efficient communication between end devices and network resources.
