This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include ISR4331 Router0, ISR4332 Router0, 3560-24RS Multilayer Switch0, 3560-24PS Multilayer Switch0, and multiple 2960-24TT Switches. These devices are connected via a combination of multilayer switches and access layer switches, facilitating both data flow and redundancy. The overall structure supports efficient communication among end devices, servers, and routers, ensuring high availability and scalability. Notable features include the distribution and access layers, which manage traffic and provide connectivity for user devices.
