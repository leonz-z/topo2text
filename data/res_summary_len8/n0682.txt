This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a router, a central switch (中心交换机), three switch groups (交换机组 1, 交换机组 2, 交换机组 3), and two servers (服务器I and 服务器II), connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among connected devices. Notable features include redundancy and centralized management, ensuring efficient communication between user groups and services.
