This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet Network, DSL Modem, Ethernet Switch/Hub, Wireless Router, and various connected devices such as the SMART TV, HD Player, Console, Computer, and smartphones/laptops. The devices are connected via wired connections to the Ethernet Switch/Hub and wireless connections through the Wireless Router. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity within the home network and to the internet. Notable features include centralized management through the Ethernet Switch/Hub and wireless access via the Wireless Router, ensuring seamless communication between all user devices.
