This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a modem, a TP-Link switch, a D-Link DIR-619 wireless router, and a server, connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between various departments, including the Design Department and Control Department. Notable features include centralized management through the TP-Link switch and wireless connectivity provided by the D-Link router, ensuring seamless communication among users and devices within the organization.
