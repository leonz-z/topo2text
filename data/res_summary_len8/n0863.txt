This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the core router (H3C AR 18-63-1), core switches (H3C S1026T), and additional switches (Ruijie RG-NBS1826CC). These components are connected via Ethernet cables, facilitating communication between various devices. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity across different floors of the school. Notable features include redundancy through multiple switches and weak current cabinets, ensuring reliable communication between office computers and all-in-one machines used for classroom activities.
