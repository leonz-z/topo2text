This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **Root Bridge**, **Switch 1**, **Switch 2**, and the **Rendezvous Point (RP)**, connected via **Gigabit Ethernet links** (Gi1/2, Gi1/1, Gi1/3). The overall structure supports **efficient data flow and redundancy**. Notable features include **Topology Change Notification (TCN)** segments and **Designated Port (DP)** functionality, ensuring efficient communication between the **Root Bridge**, **Switches**, and the **RP**.
