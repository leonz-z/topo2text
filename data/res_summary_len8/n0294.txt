This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include Dispatcher Workstations, Switching Units, Master *A*, Master *B*, a Front End Processor (FEP), a Terminal Server, Routers, and Remote Terminal Units (RTUs), connected via data links and switching units. The overall structure supports efficient communication and data management across various devices and systems. Notable features include redundancy through multiple masters and switching units, ensuring reliable operations and control over connected lighting and other field devices.
