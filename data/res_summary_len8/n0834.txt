This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include multiple star topologies (Star Topology 1, Star Topology 2, Star Topology 3, and Star Topology 4) and a Main Ring Topology, connected via central hubs. The overall structure supports efficient communication and data flow among various devices while enhancing redundancy and reliability. Notable features include the ability to easily add or remove devices in the star segments and the fault tolerance provided by the main ring topology, ensuring robust connectivity across the network.
