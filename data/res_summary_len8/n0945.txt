This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the North Campus Core Switch, South Campus Core Switch, North-South Campus Connection Line, DNS Server, Internal and External Servers, Safety Production Server, Internet Gateway, and South Campus Area. The nodes are connected via dedicated lines and core switches, facilitating efficient data flow and communication. The overall structure supports robust connectivity and redundancy, ensuring efficient communication between the North and South Campuses and their respective services. Notable features include high availability and scalability, which enhance the network's resilience and performance.
