This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **Layer 1 (Edge Devices)** such as computers, tablets, and robots; **Layer 2 (Edge Computing)** nodes that facilitate local data processing; and **Layer 3 (Cloud Computing)** servers providing centralized resources. The nodes are interconnected via various links, allowing for efficient data flow and communication. The overall structure supports optimized performance and resource utilization. Notable features include reduced latency and enhanced scalability, ensuring efficient communication between end-user devices and cloud services.
