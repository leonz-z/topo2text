This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Central Router, Central Management Switch, Wired Switch, POE Switch, Floor Switches, Main Authentication and Billing Server, and Device Authentication Server, connected via a combination of wired and wireless connections. The overall structure supports comprehensive data flow and management, ensuring connectivity and security across the network. Notable features include the integration of Power over Ethernet (PoE) capabilities and robust authentication mechanisms, ensuring efficient communication between wired and wireless user groups.
