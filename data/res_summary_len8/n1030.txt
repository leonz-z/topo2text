This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the 企业路由器 (enterprise router), 核心交换机 (core switch), multiple 交换机组 (switch groups), and specialized servers such as 游戏服务器 (game server), 更新服务器 (update server), and 影视服务器 (media server). The connections are primarily facilitated through switches, allowing for efficient data flow and communication. The overall structure supports organized data transfer and management across different user groups, ensuring high availability and scalability. Notable features include a centralized core switch that manages traffic and interconnectivity, enhancing network performance and reliability.
