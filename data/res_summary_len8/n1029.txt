This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the ERP server, core switch, router, PoE switch, wireless access points (AP1 and AP2), shared printer, and access switches. The devices are connected via switches and routers, facilitating both wired and wireless communication. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing across the network. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring reliable connectivity for end-user devices such as PCs and IP cameras.
