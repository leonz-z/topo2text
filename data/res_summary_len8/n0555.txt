This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **Central Server**, **S3**, **R2**, **S1**, **S2**, **PC1**, **PC2**, **PC3**, and **PC4**, connected via **routers** and **switches**. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy. Notable features include **high availability** and **scalability**, ensuring efficient communication between various user devices and services within the network.
