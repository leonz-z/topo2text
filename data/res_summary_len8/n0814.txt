This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the 光猫 (Optical Modem), 全千兆无线暗器 (Gigabit Wireless Router), and multiple LAN segments (LAN1, LAN2, LAN3, LAN4), connected via wired and wireless connections. The overall structure supports internet access, local networking, and IPTV services across multiple devices on different floors. Notable features include dedicated segments for IPTV and high-speed wireless connectivity, ensuring efficient communication between various user devices and services.
