This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **fans-jwt-auth-provider**, **fans-admin-consumer**, **fans-admin-provider**, **User**, **fans-gateway**, **fans-admin-provider2**, and the databases at **127.0.0.1:6379** (Redis) and **127.0.0.1:3306** (MySQL). The nodes are interconnected, allowing for multiple paths for data to travel between them.

The overall structure supports **efficient communication and data management** within the system. Notable features include **high availability and redundancy**, ensuring robust interaction between the **User** and various backend services.
