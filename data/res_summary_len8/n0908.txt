This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a Router, two Switches, a Scanner, a Printer, and multiple computers connected to the network. The devices are connected via Ethernet cables to the Switches, which in turn connect to the Router. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication within the local network and between the internal network and the Internet. Notable features include centralized management of network traffic and the ability to connect various devices, ensuring efficient communication between user groups and services.
