This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include routers (R1, R3), switches (S2, S4, S5), servers (Server0, Server1), and wireless routers (Linksys-WRT300N). The devices are connected via various segments, including wired connections between routers and switches, as well as connections to PCs and servers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity across the network. Notable features include redundancy through multiple connections and centralized resources, ensuring reliable communication between end-user devices and services.
