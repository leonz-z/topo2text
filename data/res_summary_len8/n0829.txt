This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Campus Network, Wireless Controller (无线AC控制器), access points Anykey2000, Anykey2002, Anykey2003, and the WBS Base Station (WBS基站), all connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity among various devices, ensuring robust communication between client devices and the internet. Notable features include wireless coverage extension and centralized management, enhancing network scalability and performance for users across the campus.
