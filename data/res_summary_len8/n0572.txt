This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include multiple Local Area Networks (LANs), switches, routers, and an Internet connection. The LANs consist of clusters of computers connected to switches, which facilitate internal communication. Routers connect these LANs to each other and to the Internet, enabling external communication. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among devices while providing access to online services. Notable features include redundancy through multiple switches and routers, ensuring high availability and scalability, which enhances communication between user groups and services.
