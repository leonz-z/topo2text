This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Sophos Firewall, ISP Router, Stacked Switches, Servers (Virtualized), and Corp Wired segment, connected via Ethernet switches. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security management. Notable features include centralized management through the Stacked Switches and robust security measures provided by the Sophos Firewall, ensuring efficient communication between wireless users, printers, and corporate devices.
