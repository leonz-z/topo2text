This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the main control center (机房) with a monitor (监视器), hard drives (硬盘), and an administrator's workstation (管理员), as well as switches (交换机) and fiber optic transceivers (光纤收发器) connected to surveillance cameras (摄像头). The overall structure supports efficient data management, communication, and video surveillance. Notable features include high-speed data transmission and redundancy, ensuring reliable communication between the monitoring system and the surveillance cameras.
