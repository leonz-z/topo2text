This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include **SW_6509_1**, **SW_6509_2**, **SW_2950_server**, **SW_2950_edge**, and several **SW_2950_fi** switches (fi1, fi5, fi7, fi9). These components are connected via **Ethernet switches**. The overall structure supports **segmented traffic flow** across various VLANs for different departments, enhancing security and performance.
