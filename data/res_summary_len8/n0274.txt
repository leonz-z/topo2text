This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include Router0, Router2, Router3, the 3560-24PS switch, and two servers (DHCP and DNS). These are connected via routers and switches, facilitating both internal and external communications. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing, while also providing internet access. Notable features include VLAN segmentation for enhanced security and performance, ensuring effective communication between end-user devices and network services.
