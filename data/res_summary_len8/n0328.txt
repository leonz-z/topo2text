This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the QFP (Quality of Service Forwarding Platform), OC-12, and various zones (ZONE NW3, ZONE SW1, ZONE NE1, ZONE SE2), connected via high-speed links and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between different regions and external networks (Bell and AT&T). Notable features include redundancy and segmentation, ensuring robust connectivity and quality of service across the network.
