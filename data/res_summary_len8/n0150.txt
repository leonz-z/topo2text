This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Check Point UTM-1, AR4640, H3C ER5200, H3C 1224, H3C ER6300, and H3C 5200, connected via routers and switches. The overall structure supports secure and efficient data flow between various internal networks and the internet. Notable features include centralized security management and multiple access points, ensuring efficient communication between different departments such as finance, design, and administration.
