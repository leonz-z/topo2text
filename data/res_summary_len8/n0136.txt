This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **Core Layer**, **Distribution Layer**, and **Access Layer**, with servers **S1** and **S2** located at the access layer. These layers are connected via **virtual Port Channels (vPCs)**, ensuring high-speed connectivity and redundancy. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between devices. Notable features include **scalability** and **high availability**, ensuring effective communication between servers and other network resources.
