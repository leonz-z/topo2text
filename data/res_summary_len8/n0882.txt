This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the VDSL Router, Linksys WRT-54Gs, Linksys SPA-2002, NAS RAID-5 Storage Device, 5-Port Fast Ethernet Switch, 8-Port Fast Ethernet Switch, and Cisco Unified 7960 IP Phone, connected via Ethernet switches and routers. The overall structure supports internet access, local networking, VoIP services, and centralized data storage. Notable features include a combination of wired and wireless connectivity, ensuring efficient communication between various user devices and services throughout the living space and bedroom.
