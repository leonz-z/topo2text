This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the central switch (中心交换机), router (路由器), firewall (防火墙), and building switches (大楼交换机) located in the North, West, South, and East buildings. These components are connected via Ethernet cables and UTP lines. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between various buildings and external networks, such as CERENET and PSTN. Notable features include redundancy and security measures, ensuring reliable connectivity and data management across the network.
