This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **spine switches**, **leaf switches**, **virtual machines (VMs)**, **firewalls (FW)**, and **storage** devices, connected via high-speed interconnections and Layer 2 overlays. The overall structure supports **efficient data transfer** and **scalability**. Notable features include **high availability** and **security measures**, ensuring efficient communication between **end devices** and **external networks** through the **MAN/WAN segment**.
