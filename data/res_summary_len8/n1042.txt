This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a firewall, a DMZ (Demilitarized Zone), a Layer 3 switch, and multiple VLANs (VLAN1 to VLAN5), connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports external connectivity, security, and efficient traffic management. Notable features include a DMZ for public-facing services and VLAN segmentation for enhanced security and performance, ensuring efficient communication between various departments and external users.
