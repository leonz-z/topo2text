This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Interpod Network (IPN), Data Center 1 – Pod 1, Data Center 2 – Pod 2, and multiple APICs (APIC 1, APIC 2, APIC 3), along with spines, leaf switches, and remote leaves. The nodes are connected via switches and routers, facilitating communication between different data center pods. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring efficient communication between servers and remote leaves across the data centers.
