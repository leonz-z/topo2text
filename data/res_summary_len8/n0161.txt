This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the SmartTab Main Station, Router and Firewall, Switch, Modem, WiFi Access Points, and Stationary POS with Printer devices. These components are connected via wired connections through the switch and wireless connections through the WiFi access points. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and transaction processing within the SmartTab POS network. Notable features include redundancy and security measures, ensuring reliable communication between the back office and front of house operations.
