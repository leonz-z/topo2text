This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Power Center, Fiber Switch, Industrial Control Devices, Workstations, and specific control units (PZ12-A1, PZ12-A2, PZ12-A3, ACR120B). These components are connected via fiber optic switches, facilitating high-speed data transmission. The overall structure supports efficient communication and reliable operation for industrial automation and control. Notable features include power management through the UPS and redundancy measures, ensuring continuous operation and effective interaction between various network entities.
