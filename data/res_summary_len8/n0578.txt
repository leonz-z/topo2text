This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include Building A – Administration and Data Center, Building B – Library and Communication Center, Building C – Life Science Learning Resource Center, Building D – Social Science and Health, Building E – Arts and Technology, and Building F – History and Geography, connected via core, distribution, and access layers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and centralized management of network services. Notable features include high-speed data transfer and policy-based connectivity, ensuring efficient communication between all buildings.
