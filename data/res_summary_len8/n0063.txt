This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Central Data Center (中心机房), Main Data Center (主干机房), Neutral Zone (中立区), and various servers such as PDM, ERP, and MES. These components are connected via switches and routers, facilitating communication between them. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and management across the network. Notable features include secure data exchange and integration of critical applications, ensuring operational continuity and enhanced decision-making capabilities.
