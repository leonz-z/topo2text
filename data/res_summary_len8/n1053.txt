This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the m0n0wall Router on Soekris Net4801, Siemens DSL Modem, Comcast Cable Modem, Netgear 24 port Gigabit switch, Netgear 8 port Gigabit switches, Linksys SPA-202 ATA, Wifi Access Point, and FreeNAS/SlimNAS. The devices are connected via Ethernet switches and routers, facilitating both wired and wireless communication.

The overall structure supports seamless data flow and redundancy between the office and house segments. Notable features include internet access through dual modems for redundancy and a robust local area network that supports VoIP services and storage solutions, ensuring efficient communication between office and home devices.
