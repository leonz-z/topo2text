This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, Firewall, Router, WiFi Router, Switch/Hub, Server, Owner's PC, Computer 1, Computer 2, Computer 3, Computer 4, Computer 5, and Agent's Laptop. These components are connected via wired and wireless connections, primarily through the Switch/Hub and WiFi Router. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and enhances security through the Firewall. Notable features include centralized management of network traffic and the ability to provide both wired and wireless connectivity, ensuring effective communication between various user devices and services.
