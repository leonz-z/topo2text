This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include **Front Desk Reception (前台接待)**, **Front Desk Cashier (前台收银)**, **Room Management Center (房务中心)**, **Engineering Department (工程部)**, **Security Department (保安部)**, **Communication Controller (通信控制器)**, and **Server (服务器)**, connected via **network cables**. The overall structure supports **efficient communication and service delivery** among various operational segments. Notable features include **centralized management** and **scalability**, ensuring effective interaction between **staff and guest rooms** for enhanced service quality.
