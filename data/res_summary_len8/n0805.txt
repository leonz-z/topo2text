This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, 核心交换 (Core Switch), OA系统 & WEB服务器 (OA System & WEB Server), 接入层交换机 (Access Layer Switch), 办公区 (Office Area), and IBT考试中心 (IBT Exam Center), connected via switches. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between various segments. Notable features include centralized management through the core switch and dedicated resources for specific functions, ensuring effective connectivity for users in the office area and exam center.
