This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **WAN (Wide Area Network)**, **Cisco 7600**, **WLAN Controllers**, and **Mesh Access Points**, connected via **wireless connections** and **routing protocols**. The overall structure supports **efficient communication and redundancy** within the wireless network. Notable features include **centralized control** through the WLAN Controllers and **improved coverage** via the distributed Mesh Access Points, ensuring efficient communication between **end devices** and the broader network.
