This network topology represents a **hybrid topology** architecture. Key components include a **gateway**, **EUREKA-A**, **EUREKA-B**, **CONFIG-SERVER**, and various data storage services such as **MySQL**, **Redis**, **Solr**, **Kafka**, and **MongoDB**. These components are connected via **HTTPS** and internal service discovery mechanisms.

The overall structure supports **dynamic service registration and discovery**, allowing efficient communication between client applications (like mobile apps and PCs) and microservices. Notable features include **centralized configuration management** and **real-time data streaming**, ensuring efficient communication between various services and databases.
