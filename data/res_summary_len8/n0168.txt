This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include R1 (Router), MS1 (Multilayer Switch), SW1, SW2, SW3 (Switches), WEB服务器 (Web Server), and 邮件服务器 (Mail Server), connected via Ethernet switches. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and external communications. Notable features include centralized traffic management through MS1 and connections to both web and email services, ensuring reliable communication between user devices and critical servers.
