This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **Internet**, **Edge/Core Leaf switches**, **Super Spine Tier**, **Spine switches**, **Leaf switches**, and **Computer/Storage pods**, connected via **Ethernet links** and **Ethernet/FCoE links**. The overall structure supports **efficient data flow and redundancy**. Notable features include **high availability and scalability**, ensuring efficient communication between **end devices and external resources**.
