This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include **Switch-P1**, **Switch-F1**, **Switch-P2**, and multiple end-user devices (PC-PT0, PC-PT1, PC-PT2, PC-PT3, PC-PT4, PC-PT5), connected via **Ethernet switches**. The overall structure supports **efficient communication** between different layers of the network and provides connectivity to end-user devices. Notable features include **segmentation** and **data forwarding capabilities**, ensuring efficient communication between **user devices** and the broader network infrastructure.
