This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, OVM1000 (central router), ADSLX-8 (modem/router), central machine room, teaching server, data center, 0A server, campus card system, and student parent bank. These components are connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource management. Notable features include VLAN segmentation for improved traffic management and security, ensuring effective communication between students, faculty, and administrative services.
