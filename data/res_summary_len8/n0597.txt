This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include core router SR1, distribution routers R1, R2, R3, R4, and various internal servers (Server-PT and 内网服务器). The connections are primarily facilitated through routers and switches, enabling both external and internal communication.

The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource access across multiple VLANs, ensuring connectivity between external networks and internal resources. Notable features include VLAN management and redundancy, enhancing the network's scalability and reliability for diverse user groups and services.
