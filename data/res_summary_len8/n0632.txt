This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a central HUB, devices in a star network configuration, and additional devices in ring and bus network segments. The connections are primarily facilitated through the HUB, which allows for centralized management and communication among devices. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy by combining the strengths of star, ring, and bus topologies. Notable features include easy troubleshooting and predictable data paths, ensuring effective communication between various user groups and services.
