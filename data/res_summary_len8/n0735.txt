This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the VE-1000/4000 video encoders, the City Public Security Control Center (市公安局控制中心), Police Station Control Centers (派出所控制中心), public areas like the Public Square (公共广场) and Entertainment Venues (娱乐处所), the Monitoring Host (监控主机), and the VE-2016 video management system. These components are connected via a central hub, facilitating communication among them. The overall structure supports effective monitoring and management of public safety operations. Notable features include data redundancy and secure communication protocols, ensuring efficient communication between law enforcement and public safety services.
