This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Monitoring Center, Huawei Router, 2621 device, Central Management Server, Monitoring Host, Storage Server, and Client Terminals, connected via a combination of wired and wireless connections, including a CDMA network. The overall structure supports real-time monitoring, data storage, and communication between various components of the surveillance system. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring efficient communication between monitoring personnel and surveillance devices.
