This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include workstations (Managing Director, Product Design, Accounting, Shipping, Sales, New), multiple printers (Laser printer, Color printer, ASCII Dot Matrix printer), and a central network switch (Netgear). The devices are connected via Ethernet cables to the Netgear switch, facilitating communication and data transfer among them. The overall structure supports efficient communication, resource sharing, and printing capabilities within the organization. Notable features include centralized management through the Netgear switch, ensuring efficient communication between users and shared resources.
