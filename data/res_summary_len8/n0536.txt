This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the GloEWR4009 and GloEWR4003 routers, ADSL routers, a monitoring center, the principal's office, the education bureau monitoring center, student parents, and a wireless network. The devices are connected via Ethernet cables and ADSL connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and management within the educational environment. Notable features include centralized monitoring and connectivity, ensuring effective communication between administrative staff, students, and parents.
