This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include a **Modem**, **router (router.mydns)**, **Mesh Router**, **Mesh Sub-Router**, and a **server (server.mydns)**. These components are connected via both wired and wireless connections, facilitating robust communication throughout the network. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy, ensuring reliable connectivity for various devices, including PCs, laptops, and mobile devices. Notable features include high availability and scalability, which enhance the network's performance and user experience.
