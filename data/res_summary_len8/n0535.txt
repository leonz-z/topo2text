This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include surveillance cameras (MLM-17, MEH-61, MFB-12, MU-939, MFB-11, MTB-15) and a DVR (MQ-2008), connected via direct links to the DVR for centralized video management. The overall structure supports comprehensive surveillance across various areas, ensuring security and monitoring of activities. Notable features include real-time monitoring and data consolidation, ensuring efficient communication between different monitoring zones such as the office area, transportation area, warehouse, installation point, and external area.
