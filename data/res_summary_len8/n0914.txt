This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the main office (北京总部公司), three firewalls (FW1, FW2, FW3), and a central switch (SW1). The connections are primarily established through secure segments between the firewalls and the switch, facilitating traffic flow and secure communication to branch offices in Zhengzhou and Shanghai. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security measures across the network. Notable features include secure tunnels for encrypted communication between sites, ensuring high availability and integrity of data transfer between the main office and its branches.
