This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the GloVIEWR4009 and GloVIEWR4005 routers, ADSL routers, a Local Monitoring Center (本地监控中心), a Remote Monitoring Center (远程监控中心), and Mobile Users (移动用户), connected via both wired and wireless connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and monitoring across the network, ensuring communication between local and remote entities. Notable features include centralized monitoring capabilities and internet connectivity, facilitating management and oversight of network activities.
