This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the main control center (机房), switches (交换机), fiber optic transceivers (光纤收发器), hard drives (硬盘), and surveillance cameras (摄像头), connected via switches and fiber optic links. The overall structure supports effective data flow and monitoring. Notable features include centralized management and efficient communication between the administrator (管理员) and various network components, ensuring robust surveillance and data management capabilities.
