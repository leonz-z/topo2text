This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include RG-MALL 1600 (gateway), SA-5020 (security appliance), S6800E (core switch), VOD Service Server, SMS Authentication Server, Application Data Server, DNS/DHCP Server, and various student segments. These components are connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring effective communication between internal services and external networks.
