This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Firewall, Router, Server, Switches, a Hub, an Access Point (AP), Workstations, a Printer, and various end-user devices such as laptops, smartphones, and tablets. These components are connected via fiber and metallic connections, with switches facilitating communication between devices. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing, while ensuring internet access and security. Notable features include redundancy through multiple switches and the use of both wired and wireless connections, ensuring reliable communication between user groups and services.
