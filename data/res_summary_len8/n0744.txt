This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a 15-port core switch, a 10-port wireless access switch, a 6-port Power over Ethernet (POE) switch, and a 2-port POE switch, all connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and wireless connectivity. Notable features include the provision of power and data through POE switches, ensuring flexible placement of devices and enhancing network accessibility for both wired and wireless users.
