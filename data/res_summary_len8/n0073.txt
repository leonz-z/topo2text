This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Campus Core Network (校园核心网), Optical Line Terminal (OLT部件在中), Wireless Controller (无线控制器), and various wired/wireless terminals (有线/无线终端). These components are connected via optical fibers and wireless connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity across the network. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring reliable communication between users and services throughout the campus environment.
