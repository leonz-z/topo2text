This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the **核心交换机 (Core Switch)**, **安全感知平台 (Security Awareness Platform)**, **SSL VPN 网关 (SSL VPN Gateway)**, **上网行为管理 (Internet Behavior Management)**, and **EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response)**, connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports secure data flow and efficient communication between various network segments. Notable features include enhanced security measures and centralized management, ensuring effective monitoring and response to potential threats across the network.
