This network topology represents a star topology architecture. Key components include the VPNZ150, AF1520, H3C WX2540H, H3C S5500, H3C S110, H3C S5800, PACS servers, and PES servers, connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and secure remote access.
