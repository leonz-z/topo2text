This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Firewall, Hub, Server, a 48-port Switch, a Wireless Router, and an additional Switch. The devices are connected via Ethernet cables and wireless connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity across various user segments, including reception, dining room, and office areas. Notable features include redundancy through multiple switches and segments, ensuring high availability and scalability, which facilitates effective communication between different user groups and services.
