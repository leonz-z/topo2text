This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the 主服务器 (Main Server), 主交换 (Main Switch), 路由 (Router), 移动网络 (Mobile Network), and 移动信令、数据中心 (Mobile Signaling and Data Center). These components are connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication across various network segments. Notable features include centralized management and mobile connectivity, ensuring effective communication between regional management terminals and mobile services.
