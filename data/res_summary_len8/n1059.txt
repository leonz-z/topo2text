This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the 大数据区域 (Big Data Area), Fleet, 核心交换机 (Core Switch), vpn-server, and ES. These components are connected via various methods, including switches and firewalls. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource management.
