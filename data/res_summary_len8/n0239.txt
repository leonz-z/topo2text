This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet (CERNET), a router, a firewall, the Huawei MA5200, the CISCO 4003 switch, Huawei S3025 switches, and Huawei 2403F switches, connected via fiber optic and copper cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security across the network. Notable features include redundancy and segmentation, ensuring efficient communication between various departments and facilities such as classrooms, laboratories, and dormitories.
