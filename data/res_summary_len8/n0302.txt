This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a router (路由器), external servers (外部服务器), terminal devices (终端设备), and an administrator (管理员), all connected via ADSL and VPN links. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and management, ensuring connectivity between end-user devices and external resources. Notable features include centralized management through the router and secure connections to external servers, facilitating reliable communication and resource accessibility for users.
