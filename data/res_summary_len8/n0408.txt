This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Local Area Network (LAN) connecting multiple computers within a limited geographical area and a Wide Area Network (WAN) that interconnects multiple LANs over larger distances. The overall structure supports efficient communication and resource sharing among devices in close proximity while enabling broader connectivity across long distances. Notable features include the integration of star topology elements in the LAN segment and mesh topology elements in the WAN segment, ensuring reliable data flow and access to external resources.
