This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Router, two switches (one labeled as "S switch"), a Server, a WiFi Router, an IP Phone, a Scanner, a Printer, and multiple PCs arranged in a ring configuration. The devices are connected via wired connections through switches and wireless connections through the WiFi Router. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between various user groups, including wired and wireless devices. Notable features include redundancy through multiple switches and a segmented approach to network traffic, ensuring efficient communication and resource access across the network.
