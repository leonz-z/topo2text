This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the LAN GbE Switch with IGMP & PoE, KE9952T (Front and Rear), KE9950R, KE9952R (Front), and CCKM Software Servers (Primary and Secondary), connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient device connectivity and power management through Power over Ethernet (PoE). Notable features include redundancy provided by the primary and secondary servers, ensuring reliable network services and communication between user terminals and management systems.
