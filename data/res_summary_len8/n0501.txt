This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, Modem, Router/Firewall, Switch, Server, Workstation, WiFi access point, and various end-user devices such as iPhones, laptops, and desktop computers, all connected via Ethernet cables and wireless connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among devices. Notable features include centralized management through the Router/Firewall and Switch, ensuring secure and reliable communication between users and services within the network.
