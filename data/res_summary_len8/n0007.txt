This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Router, a Distribution Switch, and three Access Layer Switches (Research Switch, Engineers Switch, and Sales Switch), along with a DMZ Switch hosting Web, Email, and DNS servers. These components are connected via fiber optic cables and crossover cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy. Notable features include a hierarchical design with core, distribution, and access layers, ensuring secure and organized communication between different departments and services.
