This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a bus segment connecting Node 1 and the Hub, a star segment connecting Node 2 and Node 3 to the Hub, and a ring segment connecting Nodes 4, 5, 6, and 7. The connections are facilitated via a central hub and a bus backbone. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication among the nodes. Notable features include redundancy through multiple segments and centralized management via the hub, ensuring effective communication between various user groups and services.
