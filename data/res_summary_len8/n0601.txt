This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the VoIP Service Provider, VoIP Phone, VoIP ATA, GoIP, GoIP-4, GoIP-8, Telephone (PSTN), and cellphone, connected via the Internet/Intranet and various gateways. The overall structure supports seamless voice communication across different platforms, bridging traditional telephony with VoIP services. Notable features include integration of multiple communication technologies and support for both mobile and landline connections, ensuring efficient communication between users.
