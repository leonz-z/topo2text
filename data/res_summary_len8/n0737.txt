This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include **routers (R)**, **core switches (S8505)**, **access switches (S6503)**, **CAMS (Centralized Authentication Management System)**, and various access points (WA1006E). The connections are primarily facilitated through **switches and routers**. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity across different segments, including the **Internet**, **CERNET**, **Student Dormitory Area**, **Academic Area**, and **Affiliated Middle School**. Notable features include **centralized user authentication** and **redundant connections**, ensuring secure and reliable communication between user groups and educational services.
