This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include routers (R1), switches (S1, S2, S3, S4), firewalls (FW1, FW2, FW3), and security devices (DPI, IPS). These components are connected via various devices, facilitating communication between local area networks (LAN1 and LAN2), a demilitarized zone (DMZ), and the Internet. The overall structure supports secure and efficient data flow, with notable features such as layered security through multiple firewalls and dynamic management via SDN and MB controllers, ensuring robust communication between internal and external entities.
