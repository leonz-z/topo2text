This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a ROUTER, TL-AC100 (central controller), TL-SG3226 (core switch), and multiple TL-SL1226P (POE switches). These devices are connected via Ethernet cables and Power over Ethernet (POE) connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and power distribution to connected devices. Notable features include wireless access points extending coverage to various areas, ensuring reliable communication for users across different locations.
