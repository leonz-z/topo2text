This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the switch (交换机), cameras (高清网络摄像机), IPSAN, digital matrix (数字矩阵), management server (管理服务器), monitors (监视器), and video wall (电视墙), connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports effective surveillance and monitoring. Notable features include centralized management and data storage, ensuring efficient communication between surveillance devices and display systems.
