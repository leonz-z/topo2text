This network topology represents a star topology architecture. Key components include the 光猫 (Optical Modem in Router Mode), 光猫 (Optical Modem in Bridge Mode), 无线路由器 (Wireless Router), and NAS (Network Attached Storage), connected via optical fiber and Ethernet connections. The overall structure supports internet access, local networking, and data storage capabilities. Notable features include the ability to manage local traffic and device connectivity, ensuring efficient communication between connected devices and the internet.
