This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, NAS (Network Attached Storage), AC controller, Layer 2 managed switch, POE switch, monitoring POE switch, access point panels, and NVR (Network Video Recorder). These components are connected via Ethernet switches, facilitating communication among devices. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and centralized storage management. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring effective communication between various networked devices and services.
