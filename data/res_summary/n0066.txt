This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Cisco 6509 core switch, Cisco 3640 router, Cisco 7507 core router, access switches (Cisco 2950 and 2924), ISA proxy server, and various servers (financial, trading, document, etc.). The devices are connected via Ethernet switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity among different network segments. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring effective communication between end-user devices and critical services.
