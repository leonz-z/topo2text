This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a router (路由器), external application server (外部应用服务器), hotel servers (酒店服务器), and a management station (管理者), connected via ADSL and VPN links. The overall structure supports efficient communication and data management within the hotel network and with external users. Notable features include centralized traffic management and the ability to facilitate service delivery, ensuring robust connectivity and operational efficiency.
