This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the ProSafe 8-port Gigabit Switch with 4-port PoE (GS108P), ProSafe 8-port Gigabit VPN Firewall (FVS318G), ProSafe 802.11n Dual Band Wireless Access Point (WNDAP330), ProSafe 802.11G Wireless Access Point (WG103), and ReadyNAS NVX (RNDX4250), connected via Ethernet cables and wireless connections. The overall structure supports secure internet access, internal communication, and wireless connectivity. Notable features include VLAN segmentation and the ability to manage traffic effectively, ensuring efficient communication between wired and wireless devices.
