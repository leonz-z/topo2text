This network topology represents a bus architecture. Key components include the lighting segment (street lamps), power distribution segment (DC power supplies), device connection segment (webcam, speaker, warning sensors, LED lamp), and the LED driver. These components are connected via DC power supplies. The overall structure supports functionalities such as illumination, monitoring, and alerting. Notable features include the integration of various power levels (DC12V, DC24V, DC48V) to cater to different devices, ensuring efficient communication between the lighting and monitoring systems.
