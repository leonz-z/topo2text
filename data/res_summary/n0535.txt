This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the DVR (MQ-2008), monitor (MLM-17), and various surveillance cameras (MEH-61, MFB-12, MUF-939, MFB-11, MTB-15). These components are connected via direct links to the DVR, which serves as the central hub for video management. The overall structure supports comprehensive surveillance across different areas, including office, transportation, warehouse, installation points, and external environments. Notable features include centralized video recording and monitoring, ensuring efficient communication between various surveillance segments.
