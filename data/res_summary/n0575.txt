This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **ISP modem**, **Router (Netgear)**, **Switch 1**, **Access Point 1**, **Access Point 2**, and **Switch 2**, connected via **wired connections** and **wireless links**. The overall structure supports **both wired and wireless connectivity**, ensuring efficient communication between various devices such as laptops, mobile phones, smart TVs, and network printers. Notable features include **high availability and redundancy**, enhancing network reliability and accessibility for users across the network.
