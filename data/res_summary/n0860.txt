This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the IDC (Internet Data Center), S8505 and S8512 switches, and three campus locations: 宝山校区 (Baoshan Campus), 延长校区 (Yanchang Campus), and 嘉定校区 (Jiading Campus). The nodes are connected via switches, facilitating efficient data traffic management. The overall structure supports robust data flow and connectivity between the central data center and various campus locations. Notable features include redundancy and centralized management, ensuring efficient communication between users and resources across the network.
