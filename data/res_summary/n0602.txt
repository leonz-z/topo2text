This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Internet, ADSL/Cable Modem, Wireless Router (G10VIEWR4001), LAN, and various cameras and monitoring devices. These components are connected via both wired and wireless connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between devices within the local area network and the internet. Notable features include the integration of surveillance capabilities and the ability to access video feeds remotely, ensuring effective monitoring and connectivity for users within the network.
