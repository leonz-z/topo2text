This network topology represents a star topology architecture. Key components include two Layer 3 devices (D2-1 and D2-2) serving as default gateways, and three Layer 2 switches (A2-1, A2-2, and A2-3) connected to the D2 devices. The connections are established via 802.1Q trunk links, allowing for the transmission of multiple VLANs over a single physical link. The overall structure supports efficient management of VLAN traffic and routing capabilities between different VLANs. Notable features include redundancy through dual gateways and VLAN filtering, ensuring efficient communication between various user groups and services across VLANs 120, 130, 140, 141, and 131.
