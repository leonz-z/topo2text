This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the 光猫接入 (Optical Modem), OpenWRT 主路由 (OpenWRT Main Router), 交换机 (Switch), 子路由器A (Sub-router A), and 子路由器B (Sub-router B), connected via wired and wireless connections.
