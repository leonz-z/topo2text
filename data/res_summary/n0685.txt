This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Interpod Network (IPN), multiple Application Policy Infrastructure Controllers (APIC 1, APIC 2, APIC 3), servers, remote leaf switches, and spines and leaf switches in two data centers. The nodes are connected via a combination of centralized connections through the IPN and multiple interconnections among the nodes, facilitating efficient communication.

The overall structure supports robust data flow and redundancy, ensuring high availability and scalability. Notable features include support for OSPF routing, maximum latency management, and DHCP relay, which enhance the network's performance and reliability across different data center pods and remote locations.
