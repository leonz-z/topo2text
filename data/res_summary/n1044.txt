This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a central HUB and multiple SPOKE nodes (devices) connected to it. The connections are primarily facilitated through the HUB, which acts as the central point for communication. The overall structure supports centralized management and communication, allowing for easy addition or removal of devices without disrupting the network. Notable features include failure isolation, ensuring that the failure of one SPOKE does not affect the others, thereby enhancing reliability and efficiency in communication between the connected devices.
