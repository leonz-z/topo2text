This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a router, network 110, a main switch, servers, and multiple switches connected to workstations. The connections are primarily facilitated through switches and the main switch, which manages communication between devices. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity within the local network. Notable features include centralized management through the main switch, ensuring high availability and scalability, which facilitates seamless communication between end-user devices and network resources.
