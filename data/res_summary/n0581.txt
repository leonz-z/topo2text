This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include internet routers (inet-rtr1 and inet-rtr2), outbound switches (outsw1 and outs2), firewalls (fw1 and fw2), core switches (csw1 and csw2), and access switches (asw1, asw2, and asw3), connected via various interfaces. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security, facilitating communication between internal devices and external networks. Notable features include redundancy and security measures, ensuring reliable connectivity and protection against external threats.
