This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Core Layer (with the Cisco Call Manager), Distribution Layer, Access Layer (including Local PBX, LAN, Work Group, and Local Routing), and WAN. The layers are connected via high-speed switches and policy-based switching mechanisms. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and voice communication across the network. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring effective communication between local and remote workgroups.
