This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the core switch (C6506), access switches (C3548), an aggregation switch (C4006), and segments for the information center (信息中心) and service group (服务群). The nodes are connected via a combination of star and bus configurations, facilitating efficient data flow and connectivity. The overall structure supports effective management and routing of data between users, services, and the internet. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring reliable communication between various network segments.
