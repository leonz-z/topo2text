This network topology represents a star topology architecture. Key components include the Cisco 7613 router, Cisco ASA 5585-X firewall, Linksys SR2016 switches, and various servers (Web, Email, DNS, Database, Application). The devices are connected via switches and routers, facilitating communication between the internal network and the Internet. The overall structure supports secure data flow and efficient communication among internal servers. Notable features include robust security measures provided by the Cisco ASA 5585-X, ensuring protection against external threats and maintaining the integrity of internal resources.
