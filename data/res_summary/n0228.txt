This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a central hub, devices connected in a star topology, and a bus topology segment. The connections are facilitated through a hub that manages communication between devices in the star segment, while the bus segment allows multiple devices to communicate along a shared line. The overall structure supports flexible and scalable network design, leveraging the advantages of both bus and star topologies. Notable features include centralized management through the hub and the ability to integrate multiple configurations, ensuring efficient communication between connected devices.
