This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the MSC/MGW, SGSN, GGSN, MME, and HSS, connected via various interfaces such as Gn, S3, S4, S5/S8, and S6a. The overall structure supports comprehensive mobile communication services across different generations of technology, including 2G, 3G, and 4G. Notable features include high availability and session management, ensuring efficient communication between mobile users and external services like the Internet.
