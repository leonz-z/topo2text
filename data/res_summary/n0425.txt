This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include core switches (C1, C2, C3, C4), aggregation switches (T00, T01, T10, T11, T20, T21, T30, T31, T32, T33), and edge devices (S1 to S16), connected via a combination of direct links and star configurations. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy. Notable features include high-speed data transfer and optimized traffic management, ensuring effective communication between end-user devices and the core network.
