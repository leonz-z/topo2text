This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Internet, GE (Gateway), 核心交换机 (Core Switch), 层交换机 (Layer Switch), 无线AP (Wireless Access Point), IMC智能管理中心 (Intelligent Management Center), 服务器群 (Server Cluster), and 存储中心 (Storage Center), connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and management across the network. Notable features include high availability and centralized management, ensuring efficient communication between various user groups and services.
