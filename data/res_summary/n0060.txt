This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a router (2811), multilayer switches (3560-24PS, Multilayer Switch1), Layer 2 switches (2960-24, 2950-24), and a wireless router (Linksys-WRT300N). These devices are connected via various switches and routers, facilitating both wired and wireless communication. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy. Notable features include inter-VLAN routing capabilities and wireless connectivity, ensuring seamless communication between end devices (PCs and PDAs) and the network resources.
