This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the 存储服务器 (Storage Server), 管理服务器 (Management Server), 视频流服务器 (Video Streaming Server), and 监控管理中心 (Monitoring Management Center), all connected via a TCP/IP network. The overall structure supports efficient data management, streaming, and monitoring. Notable features include centralized control through the 管理服务器, ensuring efficient communication between various servers and connected devices, such as cameras.
