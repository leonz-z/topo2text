This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the ISR4331 Router0, Multilayer Switch0, and three access layer switches (Switch0, Switch1, and Switch2), connected via Ethernet links. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity among end devices like PCs and servers. Notable features include a clear separation of layers (access, distribution, and core), ensuring high availability and scalability, facilitating seamless communication between users and services within the network.
