This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the m0n0wall Router on Soekris Net4801, Netgear 24 port Gigabit switch, Siemens DSL Modem, Comcast Cable Modem, and Linksys SPA-202 ATA, connected via underground Cat 5 cabling and various switches. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between the office and house segments. Notable features include redundancy through dual internet connections and network segmentation, ensuring reliable connectivity for both office and home devices.
