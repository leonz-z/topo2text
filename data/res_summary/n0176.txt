This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, Edge Router, 5500 Switch, and three VLANs (VLAN1, VLAN2, VLAN3), connected via Layer 2 switches. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and traffic management within the internal network while providing connectivity to the external Internet. Notable features include VLAN segmentation for improved performance and security, ensuring efficient communication between different departments or functions within the organization.
