This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the NAC3000, NF1000, VS4500-28GF, VS4100GP-18G-P switches, and P780 devices, connected via Ethernet switches. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity within the network. Notable features include a core segment managed by the NF1000 and a distribution segment facilitated by the VS4500-28GF, ensuring reliable communication between end-user devices (P780) and the internet.
