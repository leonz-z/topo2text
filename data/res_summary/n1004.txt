This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include the **Modem**, **DSA-3900**, **Billion 7400XA**, **Access Point Villa 4**, and **Access Point Bruce + Cafe**, connected via **wired connections** to the modem and DSA-3900, and **wireless connections** to the access points. The overall structure supports **data flow and wireless connectivity** for clients. Notable features include **centralized management** and **wireless coverage**, ensuring efficient communication between **users in the vicinity of the access points**.
