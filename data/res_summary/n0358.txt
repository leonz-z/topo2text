This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the core switch (核心交换机), switch (SW5), access points (AP1, AP2, AP3, AP4, AP5, AP6), and servers (服务器A, Server4, Server5), connected via switches and wireless access points. The overall structure supports wireless access for users while ensuring efficient data management and connectivity through the core switch and servers. Notable features include centralized management of data traffic and the ability to provide services to multiple user devices, ensuring efficient communication between users in various areas such as conference halls and dining areas.
