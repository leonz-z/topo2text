This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include **Router-PT**, **Server-PT**, **Switch-PT-Empty**, **Switch0**, and **Switch1**, connected via **Ethernet switches**. The overall structure supports **efficient data flow and management** across different VLANs and segments. Notable features include **segmentation for security and performance**, ensuring efficient communication between **servers and various network segments** such as VLAN1, VLAN2, VLAN3, and the DMZ.
