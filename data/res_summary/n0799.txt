This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, Firewall, multiple Servers, and several switches (交换机), connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication among various segments, including classrooms (教室), offices (办公室), libraries (图书馆), and dormitories (学生宿舍 and 教师宿舍). Notable features include centralized management through the switch and security measures provided by the Firewall, ensuring reliable access to resources for users across the network.
