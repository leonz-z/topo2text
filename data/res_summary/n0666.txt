This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include cameras, video encoders, a local area network (LAN), a monitoring center, and an internet connection. The cameras capture video feeds, which are transmitted to video encoders that convert the signals for the LAN. The LAN facilitates communication between the encoders and the monitoring center, where video feeds are processed and displayed. The overall structure supports real-time monitoring and data sharing. Notable features include remote access capabilities and a comprehensive video surveillance system, ensuring efficient communication between monitoring personnel and the video sources.
