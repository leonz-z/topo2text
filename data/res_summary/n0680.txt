This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include Cisco 3825-6, Cisco 2851-1, Cisco 3845-1, TU 128 (vrf IPVS), and TU 192 (vrf IPVS), connected via routers and a global routing table. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and external connectivity to the internet. Notable features include isolated routing for specific applications, ensuring secure and dedicated resources for different services, and facilitating communication between various sites and application-specific services such as media servers and viewing stations.
