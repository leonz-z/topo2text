This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the ADSL and CABLE segments, the GloVIEWR4001 router, the LAN, and the monitoring center (监控中心). These are connected via routers and switches. The overall structure supports internet connectivity and local network operations, particularly for surveillance purposes. Notable features include redundancy through multiple internet connection methods (ADSL and CABLE), ensuring efficient communication between local devices and external resources.
