This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a central HUB, which connects devices in a star configuration, alongside segments arranged in ring and bus topologies. The connections are primarily facilitated through the HUB, allowing for centralized communication in the star network, while the ring and bus segments serve distinct purposes. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy across different network segments. Notable features include scalability and ease of management, ensuring effective communication between various user groups and services.
