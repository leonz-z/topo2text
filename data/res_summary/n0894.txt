This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include a **modem**, **router (router.mydns)**, **mesh router**, **mesh sub-router**, and a **server (server.mydns)**, connected via both wired and wireless connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and internet access across various devices, including PCs, laptops, and mobile devices. Notable features include extended Wi-Fi coverage and redundancy, ensuring reliable communication between users and network resources.
