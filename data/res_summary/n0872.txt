This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include vManage, vSmart, vBond, and vEdge, connected via secure control channels and various transport networks such as INET, MPLS, and 4G. The overall structure supports efficient management, orchestration, and data transmission across different segments, including the orchestration, management, control, and data planes.
