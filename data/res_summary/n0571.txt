This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include the **WEB服务器 (Web Server)**, **宿舍楼1 (Dormitory Building 1)**, **宿舍楼5 (Dormitory Building 5)**, **教学楼 (Teaching Building)**, and **办公楼 (Office Building)**, connected via **routers and switches**. The overall structure supports **efficient communication and resource sharing** among different buildings and users within the network. Notable features include **centralized web services** and **network redundancy**, ensuring efficient communication between **students, faculty, and staff**.
