This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Cisco 6509 central switch, Cisco 3640 and Cisco 7507 routers, 2950 and 2924 access switches, and an ISA proxy server. The devices are connected via Ethernet switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between various servers and user workstations. Notable features include high availability and redundancy, ensuring secure communication between financial, trading, and document management services.
