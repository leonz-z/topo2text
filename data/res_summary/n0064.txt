This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the main control center (机房) with an administrator (管理员), monitor (监视器), and storage devices (硬盘), as well as switches (交换机) and fiber optic transceivers (光纤收发器) connecting various cameras (摄像头). The overall structure supports efficient data management, communication, and surveillance. Notable features include centralized control and monitoring, ensuring efficient communication between the administrator and other network entities.
