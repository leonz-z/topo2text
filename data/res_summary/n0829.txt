This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Campus Network, Wireless AC Controller, Anykey2000, Anykey2002, Anykey2003, and various wireless access points (APs). These components are connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity among devices, ensuring robust communication. Notable features include the management of wireless signals by the Wireless AC Controller and the provision of both indoor and outdoor wireless access, ensuring comprehensive coverage for users and devices within the network.
