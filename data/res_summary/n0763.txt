This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Firewall, Hub, Server, a 48-port Switch, and a Wireless Router, connected via Ethernet cables and wireless connections. The overall structure supports secure internet access, data flow, and resource sharing among various devices. Notable features include redundancy through the use of multiple switches and a focus on security, ensuring efficient communication between reception workstations, office workstations, and wireless devices in the Dining Room.
