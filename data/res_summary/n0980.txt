This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, Firewall, Router, Switch, Wireless Access Point (AP), and various devices such as laptops and printers connected via a Local Area Network (LAN). The primary connection types involve direct links from the Router to the Switch and Wireless AP, facilitating communication within the LAN and wireless devices. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security, ensuring safe access to external resources while maintaining internal network integrity. Notable features include the Firewall's role in monitoring traffic and protecting against unauthorized access, ensuring secure communication between the internal network and external entities.
