This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include AR1 and AR2 (routers), FW1 and FW2 (firewalls), LSW4 and LSW5 (core switches), LSW1, LSW2, and LSW3 (distribution switches), and STA1 to STA6 (end devices). The connections are primarily facilitated through switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow, redundancy, and security across different network segments. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring reliable communication between end users and external networks.
