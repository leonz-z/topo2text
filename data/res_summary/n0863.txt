This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the core router (H3C AR 18-63-1), core switches (H3C S1026T), and the fifth-floor switch (Ruijie RG-NBS1826CC), connected via Ethernet links. The overall structure supports connectivity for administrative and educational purposes across multiple floors of the school. Notable features include redundancy through multiple switches and the ability to manage local traffic efficiently, ensuring effective communication between office computers and all-in-one machines for classrooms on the third, fourth, fifth, and sixth floors.
