This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, Modem, Router/Firewall, Switch, Server, Workstation, and various Desktop PCs and mobile devices. These components are connected via wired and wireless connections, facilitating communication and data flow within the network. The overall structure supports efficient resource sharing and connectivity to the Internet. Notable features include security measures provided by the Router/Firewall and the ability to connect multiple devices through the Switch, ensuring reliable communication between users and services.
