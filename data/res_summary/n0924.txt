This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a central HUB, which connects various segments: a star topology, a ring topology, and a bus topology. The connections are primarily facilitated through the HUB, allowing for communication across different segments. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and interoperability among devices. Notable features include the ability to easily add or remove devices in the star segment, the organized data flow in the ring segment, and the cost-effectiveness of the bus segment, ensuring reliable communication across the network.
