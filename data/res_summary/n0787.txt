This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, Agg-S1 (Aggregation Switch), and multiple access switches (Acc-S1, Acc-S2, Acc-S3, Acc-S4). The switches are connected via Ethernet links, facilitating communication between different departments and external networks. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among various user groups, such as the R&D, Marketing, and Administration departments. Notable features include potential redundancy through link aggregation and departmental segmentation, ensuring effective connectivity and security across the network.
