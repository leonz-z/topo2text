This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Exhibition Center (展示中心), GIS Database Center (GIS数据库中心), switches (交换机), data maintenance (数据维护), and GIS servers (GIS服务器). The overall structure supports efficient data flow and accessibility among various nodes. Notable features include centralized data management and remote access capabilities, ensuring effective communication between users and GIS services across local sites and mobile offices.
