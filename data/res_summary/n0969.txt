This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the RN5000, S6500, and S6200 switches, connected via high-capacity links (20G between RN5000 and S6500, and 10G between S6500 and S6200). The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity, with the RN5000 serving as the gateway to the Internet, the S6500 managing core traffic, and the S6200 providing access to end-user devices. Notable features include high-speed connections and redundancy, ensuring reliable communication between external networks and internal users.
