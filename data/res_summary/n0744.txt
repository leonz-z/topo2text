This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a 15-port core switch, a 10-port wireless access switch, a 6-port Power over Ethernet (POE) switch, a 2-port POE switch, and multiple wireless access points. The devices are connected via Ethernet cables, facilitating efficient data flow. The overall structure supports robust connectivity and power distribution throughout the network. Notable features include wireless coverage and Power over Ethernet capabilities, ensuring efficient communication between end-user devices and network resources.
