This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the C核心交换机 (Core Switch), C核心路由器 (Core Router), C一楼实验室 (First Floor Laboratory), C一楼研发部小机框 (First Floor R&D Small Machine Frame), and C客户服务部 (Customer Service Department), connected via switches and routers. Notable features include centralized management and redundancy, ensuring effective communication between research, development, and customer service functions.
