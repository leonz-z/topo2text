This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **Server Hotel**, **Agrah**, **Portimão**, **Vil'gort**, and **Grindavik**, connected via **routers and switches**. The overall structure supports **secure and efficient communication** between various customer segments and services. Notable features include **dedicated subnets for customers**, ensuring high availability and scalability, which enhances connectivity and resource sharing among the entities.
