This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Branch Office with wireless access points, the Enterprise segment featuring a WLAN Controller, Firewall, Router, and RADIUS/AD/LDAP server, all connected via Ethernet and wireless connections. The overall structure supports secure and efficient wireless access and connectivity for users in both the Branch Office and Enterprise environments. Notable features include centralized management through the WLAN Controller, ensuring high availability and scalability, facilitating communication between various user groups and services across different SSIDs and frequency bands.
