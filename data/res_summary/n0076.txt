This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the 电影服务器 (Movie Server), 游戏更新服务器 (Game Update Server), 过滤核心 (Filtering Core), 镜像交换机 (Mirror Switch), and 主干交换机 (Core Switch), connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between various servers and user devices. Notable features include centralized management and filtering capabilities, ensuring effective communication and data management across the network.
