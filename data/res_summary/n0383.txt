This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **core routers (AR3260-R1, AR3260-R2, AR3260-R3, AR3260-R4)** and **switches (S3700-SW7, S5700-SW5, S5700-SW6)**, connected via **Ethernet and GE connections**. The overall structure supports **efficient data flow and redundancy**. Notable features include **high availability and segmentation of traffic**, ensuring efficient communication between **end devices and servers** across different VLAN segments.
