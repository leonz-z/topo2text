This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, NAS (Network Attached Storage), AC (Access Controller), various switches (including POE switches), access points (AP), and an NVR (Network Video Recorder). These components are connected via Ethernet cables, facilitating data transfer and power where applicable. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and management, ensuring connectivity for various devices within the network. Notable features include centralized management through the AC and redundancy provided by the NAS and switches, ensuring reliable communication between user devices and services.
