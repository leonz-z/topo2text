This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a central Hub, multiple devices connected in a star configuration, and additional devices arranged in a ring configuration. The devices are connected via Ethernet cables to the Hub, which facilitates communication between the star segment and the ring segment. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy by combining the advantages of both star and ring configurations. Notable features include flexibility and scalability, ensuring effective communication between various user groups and services within the network.
