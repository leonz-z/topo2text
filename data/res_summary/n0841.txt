This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **WAN (Wide Area Network)**, **Cisco 7600**, **WLAN Controller**, and **Mesh Access Points**, connected via **wireless connections** and **routing protocols**. The overall structure supports **efficient data transmission and connectivity** within the network. Notable features include **centralized management** of wireless access points and **redundant connections**, ensuring reliable communication between **users and services** throughout the network.
