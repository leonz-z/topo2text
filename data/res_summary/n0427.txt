This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include two data centers (Data Center 1 and Data Center 2), spine switches, leaf switches, and three Application Policy Infrastructure Controllers (APIC 1, APIC 2, APIC 3). These components are connected via high-speed spine switches and an Interpod Network (IPN) that facilitates communication between the data centers.

The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy, ensuring robust connectivity and management across the network. Notable features include high availability and policy-based management through the APICs, ensuring efficient communication between servers and network devices.
