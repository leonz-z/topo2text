This network topology represents a hybrid architecture connecting a branch office and an enterprise environment. Key components include a WLAN Controller, Firewall, Router, and RADIUS/AD/LDAP server, connected via wireless access points utilizing different SSIDs and frequencies (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz) based on the 802.11n and 802.11g standards. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security across the network. Notable features include traffic prioritization through distinct SSIDs for corporate, voice, and guest access, ensuring high availability and secure communication between users and network resources.
