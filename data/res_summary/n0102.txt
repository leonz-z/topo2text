This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a Router, multiple Client Devices (laptops for Joe, Sarah, Charlie, Nick, Monica, and Boss), an iPad at Reception utilizing DHCP, a Printer Office, and a Cloud service. The devices are connected via Ethernet cables to the central Router, which manages traffic and facilitates communication between the devices and the Cloud. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among users. Notable features include centralized management and ease of access to shared resources, ensuring effective communication within the Client Office in Miami.
