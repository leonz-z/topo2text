This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Firewall, which serves as the central node, and multiple routers connected directly to it, including the 2811 router for internet access. The connections are primarily through direct links to the Firewall, facilitating secure communication between the internal network segments and the internet. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and enhances security by segmenting different subnets. Notable features include the use of various internal subnets (e.g., 192.168.0.0/26, 172.31.128.0/20) for organized traffic management and enhanced security, ensuring efficient communication between internal devices and external networks.
