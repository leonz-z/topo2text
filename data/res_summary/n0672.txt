This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include cameras (光纤摄像机), video encoders (GloVIEWR1001), a local area network (LAN), a monitoring center (监控中心), and an internet gateway (互联网). The devices are connected via transmission devices that facilitate video signal transfer. The overall structure supports video surveillance, data transmission, and remote monitoring. Notable features include redundancy and secure access, ensuring efficient communication between surveillance systems and remote users.
