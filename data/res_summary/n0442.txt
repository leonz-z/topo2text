This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include the **Internet**, **Edge/Core Leaf switches**, **Super Spine Tier**, **Spine switches**, **Leaf switches**, and **Computer/Storage pods**, connected via **Ethernet and Ethernet/FCoE links**. The overall structure supports **efficient data flow, redundancy, and scalability**. Notable features include **high availability and minimized latency**, ensuring efficient communication between **external networks and internal resources**.
