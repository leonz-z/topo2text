This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include 北京 (Beijing), 中国 (China), 云南 (Yunnan), 南京 (Nanjing), 上海 (Shanghai), 沈阳 (Shenyang), 安徽 (Anhui), 芜湖 (Wuhu), and 新彊 (Xinjiang), all connected via direct links to the central node, 中国. The overall structure supports efficient communication and data exchange across various regions. Notable features include centralized management and routing of traffic, ensuring reliable connectivity between regional nodes and the central hub.
