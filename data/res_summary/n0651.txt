This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the CERNET, PSTN, router, central switch (中心交换机), building switches (大楼交换机), and the network center (网络中心, located on the third floor of the North Building). These components are connected via various links, including fiber optics and UTP cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication across different segments of the network. Notable features include redundancy and centralized management, ensuring reliable connectivity between user groups and external networks.
