This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Government affairs public cloud, Government affairs private cloud, E-Government Extranet, Operations Center, Government affairs integration platform, Shared exchange platform, Review system, and Preparatory work. These components are connected via secure channels and gateways, facilitating both public access and internal operations. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and collaboration among various government entities and external stakeholders. Notable features include enhanced security measures and interoperability, ensuring effective communication between citizens and government services.
