This network topology represents a **hybrid topology** architecture. Key components include **Cloud1**, **FW1 (Firewall 1)**, **R (Router)**, **S1 (Switch 1)**, and **AC1 (Access Controller)**, connected via **Ethernet connections**. The overall structure supports **secure communication and resource sharing** among devices while protecting against external threats. Notable features include **network segmentation through VLANs**, ensuring efficient communication between **end-user devices (STA1, STA2)** and internal resources.
