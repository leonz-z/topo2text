This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include two schools (学校A and 学校B), a central switch (交换机), electronic evaluation systems (电子评估), electronic libraries (电子库), servers (服务器), and various devices such as computers and cameras. These components are connected via Ethernet cables to the central switch, facilitating efficient data flow. The overall structure supports educational activities and assessments, ensuring effective communication between the two schools and access to external resources through the Internet. Notable features include centralized management and scalability, enhancing the network's ability to support diverse educational functions.
