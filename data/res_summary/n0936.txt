This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the HiPER 4240G router, three SG 2124F switches (A, B, C), and the HiPER 510W wireless router. These devices are connected via Ethernet cables, facilitating communication between the internet and various internal segments. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among different user groups, including management, finance, R&D, and office areas. Notable features include wireless connectivity provided by the HiPER 510W and the segmentation of the internal network, ensuring organized traffic management and enhanced security.
