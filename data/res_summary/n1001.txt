This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Core Layer, Aggregation Layer, Access Layer (from the Traditional 3-Tier Architecture), and the Spine Layer and Leaf Layer (from the 2-Tier Spine-Leaf Architecture), connected via high-speed switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow, scalability, and management within the network. Notable features include redundancy and policy-based connectivity, ensuring efficient communication between end devices and servers.
