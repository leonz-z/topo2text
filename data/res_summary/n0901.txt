This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a router, a firewall, and three hub switches (hub switch 1, hub switch 2, and hub switch 3), connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between various devices within the local area network (LAN) and external networks. Notable features include security provided by the firewall and the ability to connect multiple devices through the hub switches, ensuring reliable access to network resources and the internet.
