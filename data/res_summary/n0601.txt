This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the VoIP Service Provider, VoIP Phone, VoIP ATA, GoIP-4, and GoIP-8, connected via the Internet/Intranet and local telephone networks. The overall structure supports seamless voice communication across various platforms, bridging VoIP services with traditional telephony systems. Notable features include the integration of multiple VoIP gateways, ensuring efficient communication between VoIP devices and PSTN (Public Switched Telephone Network) telephones and cellphones.
