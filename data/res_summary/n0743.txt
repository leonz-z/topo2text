This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **OR1, MSW1, MSW2, SW1, SW2**, and end devices **PC1, PC2, PC3**, connected via **routers and multilayer switches**. The overall structure supports **efficient data flow and redundancy**. Notable features include **VLAN implementation, VRRP for high availability**, and **DHCP for IP address assignment**, ensuring efficient communication between **end devices and network segments**.
