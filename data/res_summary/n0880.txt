This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Fortinet Firewall, Cisco Router, Exchange Server, Application Server, SharePoint Server, and various switches. These devices are connected via switches and routers, facilitating both internal and external communications. The overall structure supports secure data flow and access management across different network segments, including the DMZ and VLANs. Notable features include a dedicated DMZ for external services and a Servers VLAN for critical applications, ensuring efficient communication between internal users and external resources.
