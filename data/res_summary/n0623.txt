This network topology represents a star topology architecture. Key components include the BRAS (Broadband Remote Access Server), Huawei OLT (Optical Line Terminal), ONUs (Optical Network Units), routers, and switches, connected via optical fiber and Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports broadband connectivity and efficient data flow between users and the internet. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring efficient communication between end-user devices such as computers, telephones, and surveillance systems.
