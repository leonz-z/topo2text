This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include ASW1 (Access Switch), DSW1 and DSW2 (Distribution Switches), and the Core network. These components are connected via inter-switch links and VLAN configurations. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy among different VLANs, specifically VLAN100 and VLAN200, while also facilitating communication through the VNAN1000 segment. Notable features include inter-switch connections that enhance scalability and reliability, ensuring efficient communication between end devices and network services.
