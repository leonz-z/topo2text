This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the 光猫 (Optical Modem), 路由器 (Router), 核心交换机 (Core Switch), 16层监控主机 (16th Floor Monitoring Host), Poe供电交换机 (PoE Switch), 接入交换机 (Access Switch), and 无线接入点 (Wireless Access Points). These components are connected via various switches and routers, facilitating both wired and wireless communication. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and internet access, while notable features include the integration of monitoring systems and Power over Ethernet capabilities, ensuring reliable connectivity for both stationary and mobile devices within the network.
