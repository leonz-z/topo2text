This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include computers in the Student Dormitory Network Segment (labeled A, B, and C) and a central switch, as well as computers in the Teaching Area Network Segment connected to a central server. These components are connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient communication and resource sharing among students and faculty. Notable features include centralized management of data traffic and the ability to facilitate both local and external communications, ensuring effective data flow between user groups.
