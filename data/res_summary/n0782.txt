This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Enterprise Level (ERP, PLM, WMS, BPM, PDM, 4C), Factory Level (MES and management modules), Process Level (SCADA and PLC systems), and the Electronic Platform. These components are connected via various communication interfaces, facilitating data exchange and integration across different levels.

The overall structure supports efficient operations, data flow, and management across manufacturing and enterprise environments. Notable features include real-time monitoring and control, ensuring operational efficiency and responsiveness to market demands.
