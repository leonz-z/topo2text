This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the TL-AC100 (central controller), TL-SG3226 (core switch), and multiple TL-SL1226P (POE switches), connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity across the network. Notable features include Power over Ethernet (POE) capabilities for connected devices and wireless access points (APs) extending coverage to various locations, ensuring seamless communication between users and services.
