This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the 主服务器 (main server), 数据服务器 (data server), MODEN (modem), router, and VF8000设备 (VF8000 device), connected via internal networks and the Internet. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between internal devices and external networks. Notable features include redundancy and integration with various communication services, ensuring reliable connectivity for user groups and applications.
