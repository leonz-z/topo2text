This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the TP-Link AC100 V4.0 router, TP-Link SG1210PT switches, access points (TP-Link AX1800 and AX1908GI), and the Synology NAS (DS920+). These devices are connected via Ethernet cables, with the router serving as the central hub. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between various devices and the internet. Notable features include centralized management and wireless access, ensuring effective communication between users and services within the network.
