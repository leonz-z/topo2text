This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Quality of Service Forwarding Platform (QFP), OC-12, and various zones (ZONE NW3, ZONE NE1, ZONE SE2, ZONE SW1), connected via high-speed links and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and inter-zone communication, ensuring robust connectivity with external networks (Bell and AT&T). Notable features include redundancy and traffic management capabilities, ensuring efficient communication between different regional zones.
