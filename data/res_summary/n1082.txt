This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include ISR4331 Router0, ISR4332 Router0, 3560-24RS Multilayer Switch0(1), 3560-24PS Multilayer Switch0, and multiple 2960-24TT Switches. These devices are connected via multilayer switches and routers, facilitating efficient data flow and redundancy. The overall structure supports robust communication between the distribution layer and access layer, ensuring reliable connectivity for end devices like PCs and servers. Notable features include high availability and traffic management capabilities, enhancing network performance and user access to resources.
