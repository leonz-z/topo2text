This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Backbone Switch, Maskaan Server, and Gateway Router, connected via switched Ethernet segments. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among various network segments, including Labs, Library, Student Hostels, E-Lab, Administrative and Faculty Wings, and the Executive Program Center. Notable features include centralized management through the Backbone Switch and connectivity to the Internet via the Gateway Router, ensuring effective communication and resource access across the network.
