This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the GW RTR NOC, Classroom switch, routers (R11, R12, R61, R62), and various switches (SW11, SW12, etc.), connected via Ethernet and other networking protocols. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between internal segments and external resources. Notable features include a dedicated classroom backbone for internal communication, support for desktop PCs and WiFi clients, and provisions for redundancy and security, ensuring reliable communication within the classroom environment.
