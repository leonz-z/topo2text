This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a file server, Intel 510T switch, Dlink DES-1024 or Intel 410T switches, monitoring workstations, and PCs in various laboratories and a multimedia classroom, all connected via Ethernet links. The overall structure supports data sharing, monitoring, and educational activities through interconnected workstations and switches. Notable features include efficient data transfer and centralized management, ensuring effective communication between users and resources across the network.
