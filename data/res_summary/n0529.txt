This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include the **VDSL Router**, **Linksys WRT-54GS with DD-WRT**, **Linksys SPA-2002 ATA**, **NAS RAID-5 Storage Device**, and an **8-Port Fast Ethernet Switch**, connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between various segments of the home network, including entertainment, work, and communication functionalities. Notable features include centralized management of internet connectivity and VoIP services, ensuring efficient communication between user groups such as home office devices and entertainment systems.
