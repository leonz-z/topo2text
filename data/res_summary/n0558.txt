This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the 电信机房 (Telecom Room), 安全网关 (Security Gateway), WEB服务器 (Web Server), 核心交换机 (Core Switch), and 资源服务器 (Resource Server), connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and security management across various segments, including dormitories, cafeterias, and teaching buildings. Notable features include redundancy and access control, ensuring secure communication between users and resources throughout the network.
