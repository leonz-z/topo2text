This network topology represents a **mesh topology** architecture. Key components include **spine switches**, **leaf switches**, **virtual machines (VMs)**, **firewalls (FW)**, **storage systems**, and connections to **MAN/WAN**. The spine layer consists of high-capacity switches that provide a backbone for the network, facilitating communication between leaf switches, while the leaf layer connects to end devices. The overall structure supports **high availability, scalability, and efficient data flow** within a data center environment. Notable features include **redundancy and security measures**, ensuring efficient communication between **end-user devices and external networks**.
