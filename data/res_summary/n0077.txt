This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Internet, a central router, various servers (Web Server, Data Server, Backup Server), and multiple segments for different areas such as Guest Area, Management Center, Dining Area, Entertainment Area, Bar Area, and Health Room. The connections are facilitated via switches and routers, supporting efficient data flow and service delivery. Notable features include network segmentation for security and redundancy, ensuring efficient communication between guests and management services.
