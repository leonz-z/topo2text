This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a Router, a Distribution Switch, and three Access Layer Switches (Research Switch, Engineers Switch, and Sales Switch), connected via fiber optic cables and crossover cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between different local area networks (LANs) and external services. Notable features include a centralized distribution layer that enhances network performance and connectivity, ensuring effective communication between the Research, Engineers, and Sales departments, as well as DMZ services (Web, Email, DNS servers).
