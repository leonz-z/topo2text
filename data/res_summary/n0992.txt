This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include servers, a hub switch, a router, a firewall, a Wi-Fi router, and various client devices (desktops, laptops, tablets, mobile phones, and a printer), connected via Ethernet cables and wireless connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing among devices. Notable features include centralized management through the hub switch and enhanced security via the firewall, ensuring efficient communication between clients and servers.
