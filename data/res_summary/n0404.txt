This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include industrial control devices (工控设备), fiber switches (光纤交换机), aggregation switches (汇聚交换机), ACR1202 devices, and PZ12Z-A1 switches. The nodes are connected via fiber optic cables and standard Ethernet connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity among devices, ensuring robust communication for industrial applications. Notable features include high-speed data transmission and centralized management, facilitating seamless interaction between various network segments and user workstations.
