This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the 光猫 (Optical Network Terminal), 全千兆无线路由器 (Gigabit Wireless Router), and multiple LAN segments (LAN1, LAN2, LAN3, LAN4), as well as the 电视盒子 (TV Box). These components are connected via wired and wireless connections, facilitating both internet access and IPTV services. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and local connectivity across two floors of the building. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring reliable communication between various user devices and services.
