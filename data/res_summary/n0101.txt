This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Monitoring Center Host (监控中心主机), JST DVRs, and three monitoring stations (监控所1, 监控所2, 监控所3). These are connected via the Internet, facilitating centralized management and control of surveillance data. The overall structure supports efficient surveillance and monitoring, ensuring seamless data flow and integration across various locations. Notable features include centralized data management and the ability to access video feeds remotely, enhancing the system's functionality and responsiveness.
