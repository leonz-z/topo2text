This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include two Internet Service Providers (ISP1 and ISP2), a collapsed core layer, and an access layer. The ISPs connect to the collapsed core layer, which serves as the backbone of the network, facilitating high-speed data transfer and managing traffic. The access layer connects end-user devices, allowing users to access resources and services provided by the core layer. The overall structure supports efficient communication and redundancy, ensuring reliable connectivity. Notable features include load balancing and automatic failover mechanisms, enhancing the network's resilience and performance.
