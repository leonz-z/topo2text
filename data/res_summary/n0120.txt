This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include a Router/Firewall, Modem, Server, Switch, Workstation, Desktop PCs, and Wi-Fi access points, connected via Ethernet cables and wireless connections. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between devices within the local area network (LAN) and external internet access. Notable features include centralized management through the Switch and Router/Firewall, ensuring secure and reliable communication between user devices and external networks.
