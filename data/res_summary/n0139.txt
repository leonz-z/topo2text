This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include a Firewall, Router, Server, multiple switches (Switch-1, Switch-2, Switch-3), and an Access Point, connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and communication between various devices within the local network and external internet resources. Notable features include enhanced security through the Firewall, centralized traffic management by the Router, and wireless connectivity provided by the Access Point, ensuring seamless communication between users and services throughout the network.
