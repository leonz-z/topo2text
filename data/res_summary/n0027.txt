This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the SIP Server, Routers, and endpoints A and B. The connections are established via routers, with signaling traffic indicated by red lines and media traffic indicated by blue lines. The overall structure supports efficient communication for voice services, facilitating both signaling and media transmission. Notable features include redundancy and the use of different codecs (G711 for A and G729 for B), ensuring efficient bandwidth usage and reliable communication between the SIP Server and the endpoints.
