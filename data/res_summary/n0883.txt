This network topology represents a **ring topology** architecture. Key components include the **RN6000** (gateway to the Internet), **S4500 switches** (core switches managing data traffic), **万兆服务器 (10G servers)** (high-performance servers), and **终端客户机 (client devices)** (end-user devices). These components are interconnected via high-speed connections (10G) provided by the S4500 switches. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between users, servers, and the Internet. Notable features include redundancy and load balancing through interconnected S4500 switches, ensuring reliable communication between all network entities.
