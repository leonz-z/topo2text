This network topology represents a hub-and-spoke architecture. Key components include R1-CE, R2-CE, R3-PE, R4-PE, and R5-CE, connected via GRE tunnels and direct Ethernet links. The overall structure supports efficient communication and routing between customer edge devices and provider edge routers. Notable features include the use of Virtual Routing and Forwarding (VRF) instances for traffic isolation and the implementation of backup strategies to enhance reliability, ensuring seamless connectivity between customer networks and the service provider's infrastructure.
