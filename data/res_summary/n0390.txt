This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include spine switches, leaf switches, transit leaf switches, and Application Policy Infrastructure Controllers (APICs). The spine layer provides high-speed interconnectivity between the leaf switches in both data centers, while the leaf layer connects to endpoints and aggregates traffic to the spine layer. The transit leaf layer optimizes routing between the leaf and spine switches. The APICs manage network policies and configurations, ensuring centralized management. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy. Notable features include high availability and scalability, ensuring efficient communication between data centers.
