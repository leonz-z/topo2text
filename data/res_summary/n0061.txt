This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Internet, AC3000-32 (management device), 主路由 (main router), 核心交换机 (core switch), G3224T (transmission layer switch), and G3210P (access switches). The connections are primarily facilitated through switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity across various network segments. Notable features include centralized management and redundancy, ensuring reliable communication between end devices and the internet.
