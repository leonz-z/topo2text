This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Core, Distribution, and Access layers, along with two modular sections known as Pods (Pod 1 and Pod 2). The connections are facilitated by Virtual Port Channels (vPC) between the access layer switches and the distribution layer. The overall structure supports efficient data flow, redundancy, and scalability. Notable features include high availability and load balancing, ensuring efficient communication between end devices and the core network.
