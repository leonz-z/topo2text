This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Core Switching Area (核心交换区), Aggregation Switch Area (汇聚交换机), HIS servers, PACS servers, and LIS servers, connected via switches and routers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy. Notable features include secure access through a Security Gateway (安全网关) and integration of various application servers, ensuring effective communication between healthcare services and administrative functions.
