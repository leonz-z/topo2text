This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the PIX-515E-R-BUN firewall, LS-S2403H-EI switch, multiple LS-S3026C-SI switches, and the C2950T-48-SI switch, connected via both fiber and copper cables. The overall structure supports secure data flow and efficient communication between various network segments, enhancing connectivity and security.
