This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the Aitai Router, two Aitai Switches, and multiple HiPER 510W wireless access points. These are connected via Ethernet cables and wireless connections. The overall structure supports both wired and wireless data flow, facilitating communication between various devices. Notable features include redundancy through multiple switches and access points, ensuring efficient communication between end-user devices such as laptops and external resources on the Internet.
