This network topology represents a **star topology** architecture. Key components include **SR1** (core router), **SR2** (switch router), and distribution routers **R1**, **R2**, **R3**, and **R4**, connected via **Ethernet links**. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and routing between various network segments. Notable features include **VLAN segmentation** for traffic management and **redundancy** through multiple connections, ensuring reliable communication between internal servers and external networks.
