This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the RS5300 Layer 3 switch, the RS3300 Layer 2 switch, and multiple workstations. The RS5300 connects to the RS3300, which in turn connects to the workstations. The overall structure supports organized communication and management within the network. Notable features include VLAN segmentation for business and management networks, ensuring efficient communication between workstations and servers while maintaining network security and management capabilities.
