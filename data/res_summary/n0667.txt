This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the BroadVoice Router & Phone Adapter, a router/hub/switch, and a DSL or Cable Modem, connected via Ethernet cables. The overall structure supports local area network (LAN) connectivity and wide area network (WAN) access to the Internet. Notable features include the integration of VoIP services, ensuring efficient communication between local devices such as an analog phone, laptop, and personal computer, while providing Internet access.
