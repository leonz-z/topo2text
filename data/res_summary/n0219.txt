This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the 光纤（接入模式） (fiber optic connection), 主路由（客厅） (main router), and 交换机 (switch), connected via fiber optic and Ethernet connections. The overall structure supports high-speed internet access, efficient data flow, and wireless connectivity. Notable features include redundancy and scalability, ensuring efficient communication between various devices such as smartphones, tablets, and computers throughout the network.
