This network topology represents a star architecture. Key components include the Firewall, Router, Hub Switch, Server I, Server II, and various client devices. The connections are primarily established through the Hub Switch, which facilitates communication between clients and servers. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and resource sharing within the local area network (LAN). Notable features include security measures provided by the Firewall and the Router, ensuring controlled access to the Internet and internal resources, while also allowing for scalability and redundancy in service provision.
