This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include Fronthaul, Midhaul, Backhaul, and Core, connected via various transport links such as microwave and mmWave radios. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and connectivity between different network segments, ensuring reliable communication for 4G and 5G services. Notable features include low latency and high bandwidth capabilities, facilitating seamless interaction between radio units, baseband units, and the core network.
