This network topology represents a hybrid architecture. Key components include the servers (a server, b server, c server), the Data Acquisition Front-End Processor, the Data Collection Service, and the databases (Master and Standby). These are connected via a combination of star and bus structures, with servers linked in a star configuration to the front-end processor, while the Data Collection Service and databases are connected along a bus. The overall structure supports efficient data flow and redundancy. Notable features include secure communication through a Group VPN Private Network, ensuring data integrity and availability across the network.
