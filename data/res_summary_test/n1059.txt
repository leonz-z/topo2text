This network employs a hybrid architecture, primarily designed to optimize data management and ensure secure access. Its core nodes include: 大数据区域 (Big Data Area), Fleet, 访问Fleet (Access Fleet), 核心交换机 (Core Switch), vpn-server, and ES, interconnected and collaborating closely. For instance, 大数据区域 connects to 核心交换机, facilitating data flow; Fleet nodes are interconnected and also connect to 访问Fleet, ensuring efficient workload distribution; 访问Fleet connects to 核心交换机, which directs traffic to the vpn-server for secure remote access; and osquery nodes connect to 数据存储 (Data Storage), which in turn connects to ES, enabling data querying and analysis. These nodes are linked through data dependencies, control dependencies, and collaborative dependencies. Overall, this topology aims to optimize data management while ensuring secure access for users, making it suitable for environments that require both efficiency and security.
