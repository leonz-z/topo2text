This network employs a hybrid architecture, primarily designed to facilitate secure and efficient communication within an organization. Its core nodes include: The Internet, firewalls, a router, servers, switches, a wireless access point, and networked computers, interconnected and collaborating closely. For instance, The Internet connects to the first Firewall, which filters incoming and outgoing data; the first Firewall connects to the Router, directing traffic to the Server and the second Firewall. The second Firewall connects to a Switch, which manages data flow to another Switch. The first Switch connects to the Wireless Access Point and Networked Computers, allowing for both wired and wireless connectivity. The Wireless Access Point further connects to additional Networked Computers, ensuring comprehensive access. These nodes are linked through data dependencies and collaborative dependencies. Overall, this topology aims to provide a clear visual representation of the network's structure, ensuring secure and efficient communication while enabling effective management of the infrastructure.
